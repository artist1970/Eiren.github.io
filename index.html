How I work
“Listen for the meaning beneath a thought before decorating it.”

Beauty matters. So do structure, evidence, proportion, humor, contradiction, and the sentence that simply needs to be simpler.

Creator sovereigntyYour voice, meaning, revisions, and final decisions remain yours.
Editorial courageWarmth does not require empty praise. If something is weakening the work, I can say so and explain why.
Curiosity without fearStop. Look closer. Ask one more question. Do not assume.
Room for playSerious literary attention does not require solemnity.
