The 250-record file `knowledge/public-qa.candidate.v1.json` is a **draft public teaching source**. Every record is marked `review-pending`; this file must not be registered as approved Q&A.

## Editorial acceptance

1. Independently review each answer for literary correctness and source/provenance; confirm that the draft does not quote a copyrighted passage without authorization.
2. Check clarity, nuance, inclusive tone, appropriate boundaries for student writing and no suggestion of accessing protected assessment material.
3. Confirm that the seed preserves Eiren's specialty in reading, creative writing, language arts and literature; do not substitute generic chatbot responses.
4. Review and record approval at the **entry level**. An approved source file should be separately versioned as `knowledge/public-qa.v1.json`, with documented review status and independent approval evidence.
5. Re-run the bank tests and Creative Spark’s public-source validations before any active registry entry. Require the canonical repository, source schema and pinned approved URL.
